What you can prove

Trust is not a promise. It is a structural property.

Every guarantee below holds without anyone having to take our word for it.

  • A secret ballot

    Identity and ballot are separated by the system itself, not by policy. Nobody can link a vote to a voter.

  • A result anyone can check

    Any member, observer or auditor can verify the full count without seeing a single individual vote.

  • Legal evidence

    Every election closes with an audit trail, a certified results document and a public proof your legal team can rely on.

FAQ

Questions organizations ask us

Do voters need to install an app or create an account?
No. Voters open a link, identify themselves with the details your organization already has, and vote from any browser.
How do I know the result hasn't been tampered with?
Every vote is recorded on a public ledger. Anyone can recount the result and confirm their own vote was counted, without revealing how they voted.
Is it GDPR compliant?
Yes. Data is hosted in the EU, processing is covered by a data processing agreement, and the voter register is minimised.
